## York Space Systems (subject company)

### Corporate history and structure

- Primary operating subsidiary York Space Systems, LLC founded 2012 "to create an innovative space technology mission prime, with a goal of meeting the evolving national security threats from space by providing mission-critical spacecraft at scale, faster, and at lower cost." Headquartered Greenwood Village (Denver), Colorado. (V: York 10-Q via SEC EDGAR, 2026; Canaccord Genuity transaction page 2026-01)
- Prior to 2026-01-28 the parent operated as Yellowstone Midco Holdings II, LLC (AE Industrial Partners structure); converted to Delaware corporation York Space Systems Inc. on 2026-01-28. (V: York 10-Q, SEC)
- **IPO: began trading NYSE under ticker YSS on 2026-01-29.** 18.5M shares at $34.00; net proceeds $582.6M. Post-IPO total liquidity $895.4M (as of 2026-01-31, incl. undrawn $150M revolver). (V: SpaceNews 2026-01-29; York FY2025 results release)
- Leadership: CEO Dirk Wallinger; CFO Kevin Messerle; Chairman Charles Beames (2023 source); Melanie Preisser, EVP and GM (2024 source); Michelle LeMar, VP (2026 IDIQ release). (V: York releases; SatNews)
- FY2025 results: revenue $386M (+52% YoY, +$133M, driven by two T2TL contracts); gross margin 19.5% (+6.8 pts); gross profit $75M (+133%); $319M backlog converted to revenue; year-end backlog $543M; cash $162.6M at 2025-12-31. 2026 revenue guidance $545-595M, >70% from existing backlog at midpoint; expects positive adjusted EBITDA in 2026. (V: York FY2025 results, ir.yorkspacesystems.com, 2026)
- 10-Q risk factors explicitly flag: customer concentration ("in particular our largest customer, the Space Development Agency"). (V: SEC 10-Q)

### Products and capabilities

- **Three satellite platforms sharing a common modular architecture** (M-CLASS shares ~75% of technology with S-CLASS and LX-CLASS buses, streamlining the supply chain): (V: yorkspacesystems.com/platforms)
  - **S-CLASS:** 3-axis stabilized, mass-manufactured for constellations; ESPA-compatible; 24" x 24" stowed; 85+ kg payload; 500 W peak power extensible to 1 kW; all industry-standard payload data interfaces.
  - **LX-CLASS:** 3-axis stabilized; ESPA/ESPA-Grande; 34" x 34" stowed; 300+ kg payload; 1.5 kW peak power.
  - **M-CLASS (introduced 2025):** 1,000+ kg payload; 8+ kW peak power; "same capabilities of competitors at half the cost" (company claim); broadens York into higher-power missions without redesign.
- **Vertically integrated full stack:** design, production, integration, and operation of spacecraft; turnkey constellation management through the mission lifecycle; Mission Operations Command and Control (C2) layer. (V: 10-Q; SpaceNews)
- Unique demonstrated capabilities claimed in FY2025 release: only provider to demonstrate Link 16 from space; demonstrated in-plane and cross-vendor optical communications; delivered Dragoon mission from contract to orbit in ~7 months (75% reduction vs typical 30-month programs); NASA BARD mission executed 100+ demonstrations validating commercially procured communications. (V: York FY2025 results)
- Claimed cost position: "approximately half the cost of our competitors" (Wallinger, FY2025 release). (V, company claim)

### SDA contract book (the franchise)

- **Tranche 0 Transport Layer:** $94M award (Aug 2020) for 10 satellites; developed, delivered, and launched in ~2.5 years (company cites nine launched in its 2024 release). (V: York PR 2024-08-16)
- **Tranche 1 Transport Layer:** 42 satellites (selected Feb 2022 alongside Lockheed Martin and Northrop Grumman, each 42). York delivered 21 T1TL satellites to orbit in 2025, first prime to execute an on-orbit delivery under Tranche 1, a month ahead of its nearest competitor. (V: SpaceNews 2023-08; York FY2025 results)
- **T1DES (Tranche 1 Demonstration and Experimentation System):** $200M OTA (2022) for 12 experimental satellites (TACSATCOM, advanced waveforms, IBS). First, "Dragoon," launched June 2025 on Transporter-14, accelerated in response to an identified agency need; remaining 11 target FY2026. (V: DefenseScoop 2025-06-24)
- **Tranche 2 Transport Layer Alpha:** ~$617M firm-fixed-price OTA (signed 2023-10-10) for 62 satellites in eight orbital planes plus ground and five years O&S. Northrop Grumman won the other 38 Alpha satellites ($732M). Alpha carries optical inter-satellite links, Ka-band, and beyond-line-of-sight Link 16; launches from late 2026. (V: SDA.mil 2023-10-30; CNBC; Via Satellite)
- **Tranche 2 Transport Layer Gamma:** 10 satellites (awarded Aug 2024), launch 2027, supporting JADC2. (V: York PR 2024-08-16)
- **Total: 126 satellites concentrated in the SDA Transport Layer pathway** (10 + 42 + 12 + 62 as counted by SpaceNews; plus 10 T2TL-Gamma per York PR). (V: SpaceNews 2026; York PR)
- **May 2026:** multiple IDIQ awards across two national-security mission areas, including systems relevant to "emerging, highly integrated missions, like Golden Dome"; programs not publicly identified. (V: York IR 2026-05-06; SpaceNews notes the awards are undisclosed)

### Acquisitions (last 24 months bolded; earlier for context)

- Emergent Space Technologies (June 2023): mission software and GN&C for multi-spacecraft missions; 20-year heritage. (V: York PR 2023-06-07)
- **ATLAS Space Operations (announced 2025-07-18; completed 2025):** Ground Software as a Service (GSaaS) pioneer; Freedom software platform; global ground station network; acquired to eliminate ground communications bottlenecks and integrate space-to-ground connectivity; key role in York's Golden Dome architecture; operates independently under its brand. Was pending FCC approval at announcement. (V: York PR 2025-07-18; FY2025 results)
- **Orbion Space Technology (2026-03-12):** Michigan-based manufacturer of flight-proven Hall-effect electric propulsion; brings propulsion in-house, strengthens US supply chain. (V: BusinessWire 2026-03-12)
- **Solestial (closed 2026-06-04, ~$67M cash + 1,703,577 YSS shares at $34.00):** Tempe, AZ silicon space solar cells that self-anneal radiation damage; ~95% US-based supply chain; addresses China's control of ~99% of gallium and >60% of germanium needed for legacy III-V cells; legacy III-V lead times exceed two years; operates as wholly owned subsidiary, continues external sales. (V: York IR 2026-06-04; 8-K/A; Investing.com)
- **All.Space (closed 2026-07-08, ~$300M: ~$155M cash + 5.9M YSS shares):** multi-link, multi-orbit, anti-jam lens-array ESA ground terminals; completes the tactical ecosystem (spacecraft + C2 + terminals) for assured comms and positioning in GPS-denied/jammed environments; All.Space continues as merchant supplier. See the All.Space research log for full detail. (V: Via Satellite 2026-07-08; GovConWire)

### Strategic environment and risks

- **SDA restructuring (the central strategic risk):** the Pentagon is transitioning SDA's Transport Layer into the broader Space Data Network (SDN); SDA loses semi-autonomous acquisition status, programs move under Portfolio Acquisition Executives (PAEs). Transport Layer becomes "an enclave within the Space Data Network." FY2027 request: ~$1.5B for the SDN backbone incl. $685M to accelerate a pLEO mesh constellation and $800M to on-ramp additional vendors. York's incumbent advantage may erode in a broader, more competitive procurement centered on integrating military and commercial comms. (V: SpaceNews "York Space defends growth strategy as SDA programs are reshaped," 2026)
- **SpaceX won the SDN backbone:** $2.29B Space Force contract to build the Space Data Network backbone (formerly MILNET) using Starshield; operated by the Space Force; fully operational prototype required by end of 2028. This is the successor architecture to the Transport Layer York built its book on. (V: SpaceNews 2026)
- Golden Dome: $175B layered missile defense initiative; increased $10B partly for the space data network; York positions via IDIQs, ATLAS ground, and All.Space terminals. (V: SpaceNews; Defense Daily; York IR)
- Concentration: 126 satellites in one acquisition pathway; SDA is the largest customer (10-Q risk factor). Tranche 1 software issues referenced in trade coverage. (V: SpaceNews; spacedaily analysis 2026)

## Competitors of York Space Systems (ranked by threat)

### 1. SpaceX (Starshield) - CRITICAL

- Starshield: purpose-built LEO satellites for US/allied military; adapted from Starlink; target tracking, optical/radio reconnaissance, missile warning; 183+ Starshield satellites launched as of 2025; $1.8B classified NRO constellation (hundreds of spy satellites, built with Northrop Grumman as partner), operational from May 2024. (V: Wikipedia/Starshield sourced to Reuters; NROL-146)
- **$2.29B Space Force contract for the Space Data Network backbone** (formerly MILNET): LEO mesh military data network built on Starshield, operated by the Space Force, fully operational prototype by end-2028. Directly supersedes the SDA Transport Layer environment York's book was built in. (V: SpaceNews 2026)
- **$4.16B Space Force contract for AMTI** (air moving target indicator): proliferated LEO constellation to track airborne targets, initial capability by 2028; FY2027 budget seeks $7.1B for AMTI. (V: SpaceNews 2026; The Verge)
- Among 12 firms awarded Golden Dome space-based interceptor prototype OTAs (April 2026, up to $3.2B pool). Also 8 T0 Tracking Layer satellites heritage (2020). SpaceX filed for what could be the biggest IPO ever (2026). (V: Contrary Research; The Verge; Wikipedia)
- Threat model: vertical integration (launch + bus + network + ground) at unmatched scale and cost; captures the successor architecture to York's core franchise; not dependent on SDA-style procurement.

### 2. Lockheed Martin Space (incl. Terran Orbital) - CRITICAL

- SDA book: 42 T1TL satellites; 36 T2TL-Beta ($816M, Aug 2023); Tranche 2 Tracking (18, Jan 2024 award cohort); **18 T3TRK MWTD satellites ($1.1B, Dec 2025, the largest T3 award)**; company states it is on contract for 124 SDA space vehicles; launched 21 T1TL satellites with 21 more in production. SmallSat Processing & Delivery Center in Colorado. (V: SDA.mil 2025-12-19; Orbital Today 2025-12-22; Breaking Defense 2023-08)
- **Terran Orbital (wholly owned since late 2024):** high-rate smallsat bus manufacturer (Irvine, CA; 350k sqft "Factory of the Future"; Ambassador and Renegade buses). Delivered all 42 T1TL buses to Lockheed (Sep 2025); assembling 42 T2TL buses; selected Dec 2025 to provide 18 T3TRK buses. CEO Peter Krauss. (V: Terran Orbital PRs 2025-09-23, 2025-12-30; SatNews 2026-03-08)
- Threat model: prime scale + captive high-rate bus production + wins in every SDA competition York enters; the only competitor matching York's Transport+Tracking breadth with deeper pockets.

### 3. Northrop Grumman Space Systems - CRITICAL

- SDA book: 42 T1TL; 14 T1TRK (Jul 2022); 36 T2TL-Beta ($733M, Aug 2023); **38 T2TL-Alpha ($732M, Oct 2023) - the direct split with York's 62 Alpha satellites**; 18 T3TRK MW/MT ($764M, Dec 2025). 132+ SDA satellites ordered by late 2023. (V: SDA.mil; SpaceNews 2023-10-30; CNBC)
- Golden Dome: one of 12 SBI prototype awardees (April 2026); partnered with Apex (announced 2026-06-01) for a 2027 on-orbit SBI demonstration, building on a claimed $1B company-led investment in missile-defense tech. Also builds Starshield NRO satellites with SpaceX. (V: SpaceNews 2026-06; Breaking Defense 2026-06; Wikipedia/Starshield)
- Threat model: head-to-head on York's flagship program (T2TL-Alpha) with prime-scale resources; spans transport, tracking, interceptors, and payload partnerships.

### 4. Rocket Lab (National Security / Space Systems) - HIGH

- **$1.3B+ SDA prime book:** 18 T2TL-Beta ($515M, Jan 2025, third Beta performer); **18 T3TRK MWTD ($805M per SDA / ~$816M per company, Dec 2025, its largest contract)**; passed TRKT3 SRR May 2026. Lightning bus selected for both; Phoenix IR sensor payload; StarLite protection sensors; InterMission ground software. (V: SDA.mil 2025; Rocket Lab IR 2025-12-19; Markets Insider 2026-05-27)
- Vertical integration: Electron/HASTE launch; Neutron medium-lift (first launch slipped to ~Q4 2026 after a Stage 1 tank rupture in January 2026 testing); components (reaction wheels, star trackers, separation systems, solar via Solaero heritage); acquired Geost (EO/IR payloads) and Mynaric (laser optical terminals) in 2025; Flatellite stackable constellation bus (Feb 2025). $190M MACH-TB 2.0 hypersonic test contract (Mar 2026); Victus Haze responsive-space mission launched in under 17 hours (June 2026, $32M). (V: Rocket Lab releases; aviation outlook report 2026; SpaceNews)
- Threat model: the closest "new prime" analog to York with a launch segment York lacks; in-house payloads and OCTs deepen margin capture; won the T3TRK competition where York is absent.

### 5. L3Harris Technologies - HIGH

- Tracking Layer incumbent across every tranche: 4 T0 satellites on orbit; 14 T1TRK; 18 T2TRK (Jan 2024 cohort with Lockheed and Sierra Space, $919M-class award); **18 T3TRK MW/MT ($843M, Dec 2025)**; 34 satellites in development across T1/T2; expanded Palm Bay, FL satellite integration facility. Supplies FOO Fighter IR payloads to Millennium Space. (V: L3Harris PR 2025-12-19; SpaceNews; Via Satellite 2025-04-07)
- Also owns the protected-waveform modem layer (WAM, A3M) and DEUCSI multi-orbit terminal work (see the All.Space log); Aerojet Rocketdyne propulsion (2023) makes it a supplier to many satellite primes. (V: prior research log; public record)
- Threat model vs York: dominant on the Tracking side of PWSA where York has no prime position; prime scale; also a partner/supplier (frenemy dynamics repeat at the York level).

### 6. Boeing / Millennium Space Systems - HIGH

- Small satellite prime (El Segundo; founded 2001; Boeing subsidiary since 2018; ~80% vertically integrated; LEO/MEO/GEO). CEO Tony Gingiss (from Dec 2024). (V: Millennium PR; SpaceNews)
- **FOO Fighter (Fire-control On Orbit-support to the Warfighter): $414M SDA OTA (Apr 2024) for 8 fire-control-quality missile-tracking satellites + ground + mission ops;** CDR completed in 10 months (Apr 2025); delivery/launch window 2026 to late 2027 per program updates; L3Harris supplies sensor payloads. (V: DefenseScoop 2024-04-30; Millennium PR 2025-04-07; SpaceNews 2025-04-07)
- ~$1B order for 12 MEO missile-tracking satellites (US Space Force Resilient MW/MT MEO); additional classified contracts; scaling production from 1-2 to 6-12 satellites/month; new 17,000 sqft FOO Fighter facility. (V: SpaceNews "Boeing's Millennium Space expands production")
- Threat model: agile smallsat prime with fire-control heritage and Boeing capital, strongest in the missile-defense mission set Golden Dome funds.

### 7. Sierra Space - MEDIUM-HIGH

- Defense-tech pivot: Sierra Space Defense chartered June 2025; **$1.5B in national security contracts since 2023 for 30 satellites, incl. 18 T2TRK missile warning/tracking satellites (max value $740M, Jan 2024)** and a $450M award for 4+ satellites for a National Security customer; Space Force R-GPS "Quick Start" (Sep 2024). (V: Sierra Space PR 2025-06-11; BusinessWire 2026-03-05)
- Execution: completed first nine T2TRK satellite structures three months ahead of schedule (Jan 2026); Victory Works high-rate facility (Centennial, CO, 60k sqft); Eclipse bus line; Ghost re-entry delivery system; Spectre RPO satellite. **$550M Series C at $8B valuation (Mar 2026).** Dream Chaser demo flight planned late 2026. (V: Sierra Space PRs; BusinessWire)
- Threat model: well-capitalized Colorado neighbor converting commercial heritage into tracking-layer primes and natsec awards.

### 8. Apex Space - MEDIUM-HIGH (rising)

- LA-based standardized bus manufacturer (founded 2022; Aries 100kg-class, Nova, Comet, GEO Aries; Factory One). **$720M+ raised; June 2026 round of $200M+ at $2.3B valuation** (third ~$200M round in 14 months). (V: Ex Terra 2026-06; Contrary Research)
- Defense: $45.9M Space Systems Command contract (Feb 2025, deliveries through 2032, multiple orbits); Anduril partnership (AI-powered space awareness/missile tracking); **Project Shadow: self-funded $15M space-based-interceptor demo ("Orbital Magazine" on a Nova bus), launch June 2026;** Northrop Grumman SBI partnership (June 2026) for a 2027 demo. Not among the 12 April 2026 SBI prototype awardees. (V: SpaceNews 2025-02-12, 2026-06; Breaking Defense 2026-06; Contrary Research)
- Threat model: commoditized-bus insurgent aligned to Golden Dome constellation-scale production; primes (incl. Northrop) arming it with system roles.

### 9. K2 Space - MEDIUM (rising)

- Torrance, CA (founded 2022 by ex-SpaceX brothers Karan and Neel Kunjur). "Mega Class" large high-power satellites (~20 kW, 2-ton, 40 m arrays) at disruptive cost; dual-channel Hall thrusters ("most powerful currently flying"); 85% built in-house; 180k sqft factory sized for ~100 satellites/year by 2030. (V: TechCrunch 2026-03-19; 425business 2026-07)
- **$450-500M+ raised; $250M Series C at $3B valuation (Dec 2025); $500M-$1B+ in signed contracts** incl. a Pentagon program of record as bus provider for Protected Tactical Satcom-Global (PTS-G) and 28 satellites for SES's future MEO network. First satellite Heritage (Jan 2025); **Gravitas launched 2026-03-30**, 100 days of operations with 13 payloads (incl. DoD). 11 launches planned in two years; operational constellations from 2028. (V: PRNewswire 2025-12-11; K2 releases; DCD)
- Threat model: different sweet spot (large, high-power) but converging on the same DoD money (PTS-G, Golden Dome-era power-hungry payloads); M-CLASS overlaps it directly.

### 10. Muon Space - MEDIUM (rising)

- Mountain View end-to-end space systems company (founded 2021); Halo tech stack (spacecraft + payload integration + software-defined orchestration); >100% YoY growth two consecutive years; doubled headcount; $146M Series B (led by Congruent Ventures and Activate Capital); acquired propulsion startup Starlight Engines (zinc-based thrusters). (V: Muon PRs 2026-01-20, 2026-02-04; SatNews)
- Defense: **selected for MDA SHIELD IDIQ ($151B ceiling, multi-award, Dec 2025/Jan 2026)**; $44.6M Space Force SBIR Phase III OTA (Oct 2025) for a 3-satellite dual-use environmental monitoring demo (Quickbeam payloads); NRO Stage II EO contract (2025); $1.9M SpaceWERX D2P2 supporting SDA MW/MT. Commercial: FireSat wildfire constellation (protoflight 2025; 3 operational satellites mid-2026); SNC Vindler 2.0 RF constellation (Q1 2026). (V: PRNewswire; HigherGov IDIQ record; SpaceNews 2025-10-08)
- Threat model: software-led constellation prime entering missile-defense sensing via SHIELD; small today, compounding fast.

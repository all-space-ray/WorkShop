# Source: Raymond Tayse email voice samples (Fable 5 Max extraction)

## TL;DR

A second AI harness (Fable 5 Max, with mailbox access) read the same roughly 2.5 years of Raymond Tayse's sent company email and extracted 65 verbatim, redacted samples plus its own pattern analysis. It is the companion to the [first extraction](2026-07-11-voice-samples-email.md) (Claude.ai, 52 samples). The two agree strongly on the core voice and differ mainly in coverage: this run captures more one-word and no-greeting internal replies and names several grammatical fingerprints the first run did not.

## Key points

- 65 samples, dated approximately 2024-02 to 2026-07, tagged with register, audience, intent, length, and authored-by-me (64 yes; sample overlaps the first set on several messages, e.g. the "I like how you are thinking but" note and the "Add8ng ... for validation" reply).
- Retains broad institution names (US Navy, USAF, US Army, GSA, the Hill) for readability while redacting individuals, programs, products, and amounts.
- Notes a role change: "Vice President, USG Business Development" to "Vice President, Product" around mid-2025; samples span both.

## What it confirmed (agreement with the first extraction)

- "With that said" as the single most identifiable connector; affirm-then-redirect on disagreement ("I like how you are thinking but..."); answer-first then caveat ("Absolutely.", "It does.", "That is correct.").
- Semicolons, trailing "etc...", free-standing ellipses, parentheticals for roles and caveats, quotation marks around loaded terms; no em dashes; minimal or absent sign-offs; never "Best/Regards".
- Slash-joined greetings, "Brother Rick", "ALLCON", "All,"; invites correction and loops in an authority "for validation"; threads personal life in matter-of-factly; the integrity beat ("unless we straight out lie"); typos left in; no emoji; exclamation only for wins.

## What it added or sharpened (folded into the profile)

- Internal peers often get no greeting at all; he starts mid-thought. External/customers/vendors get a proper greeting. (Reconciles the first profile's "almost always a greeting".)
- A large share of output is one-word or one-line ("Respond", "Attached", "Absolutely", "FYSA", "CUI, not for distro.").
- Grammatical fingerprint: he almost never contracts negations ("do not", "cannot", "will not", "does not").
- Habitual defense/gov shorthand used without explanation: FYSA, ALLCON, CUI, ARO, POC, CR, plus-up, "the Hill", NOS.
- "heh" is his laugh marker (distinct from the first run's "Hahaha").
- Customer-perception framing as a recurring move ("Customers are going to ask", "optics are not great", "to not confuse sailors").
- Commercial-bluntness idioms: "free margin", "selling a lot of futures", "use or lose".
- Warmth tracks relationship length, not seniority (warmest note is to a senior Navy customer).

# Viasat

> **Threat rank: 8 of 10 (MEDIUM, coopetition).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Carlsbad, CA. A vertically integrated satellite operator and hardware maker: it owns Ka-band networks (Global Xpress via Inmarsat, ViaSat-3) and also builds terminals and modems. To All.Space it is simultaneously a network partner (Hydra targets GX/Viasat-3 certification) and a competitor that can favor its own hardware and controls the Ka capacity All.Space rides. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Networks:** Global Xpress (GX) and ViaSat-3 Ka-band; broad government and mobility service business. (V)
- **Terminals and modems:** builds/certifies terminals; CBM-400 software-defined modem is WGS-certified. Owns waveform/modem IP (EBEM heritage in the wider DoD Ka ecosystem). (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium (coopetition):** Viasat controls networks that All.Space terminals connect to, and can bundle or prefer its own terminals; it competes at the system/service level more than as a pure ESA terminal maker. Its leverage is capacity + bundling, not antenna architecture.
- **All.Space counter-position:** All.Space is deliberately network-agnostic and multi-orbit, which is the antithesis of single-network lock-in; being certified on GX/Viasat-3 turns Viasat into a channel as much as a rival. Watch whether Viasat pushes proprietary terminal bundles that exclude third-party antennas. (I)

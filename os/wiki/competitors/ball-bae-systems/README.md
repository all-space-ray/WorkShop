# Ball Aerospace / BAE Systems (Space & Mission Systems)

> **Threat rank: 5 of 10 (MEDIUM-HIGH).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Boulder, CO. Ball Aerospace was acquired by BAE Systems in 2024 and operates as BAE Systems Space & Mission Systems. Supplies ESA tile technology and multi-orbit electronically steered antennas at defense-prime scale. More of a tile-technology supplier and integrator than a merchant tactical-terminal vendor, but the prime-scale reach makes it a structural competitor. (V)

## Technology and products

- **ESA tiles:** building-block phased-array tiles used in partner terminals (for example, Stellar Blu's multi-orbit multi-beam aero ESA is built on BAE/Ball tiles). (V)
- **Ku-band multi-orbit ESA:** for mobile platforms, with dynamic LEO/MEO/GEO switching to optimize link performance. (V)
- Defense-prime systems integration, mission systems, and space payload heritage. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium-high:** prime-scale defense relationships, deep RF/ESA engineering, and a tile ecosystem that can arm multiple terminal makers (raising the whole field's capability). 
- **All.Space counter-position:** BAE/Ball competes more at the tile/component and integrator layer than as a fielded multi-orbit tactical terminal brand; All.Space owns a differentiated end-terminal architecture. Watch for BAE-branded end terminals or exclusive tile deals that strengthen a direct rival. (I)

# Northrop Grumman Space Systems

> **York threat rank: 3 of 10 (CRITICAL).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Defense prime with 132+ SDA satellites ordered and the **direct counterpart on York's flagship program: Northrop holds the other 38 T2TL-Alpha satellites ($732M) alongside York's 62 ($617M)**. Every Alpha launch and milestone invites a head-to-head comparison. Northrop spans transport, tracking, interceptors, and even builds Starshield satellites with SpaceX. (V)

## Technology and products

- SDA satellite lines (Redondo Beach): T1TL (42), T1TRK (14, Jul 2022), T2TL-Beta (36, $733M), **T2TL-Alpha (38, $732M)**, T3TRK (18 MW/MT, $764M, Dec 2025). (V)
- Golden Dome: one of 12 SBI prototype awardees (April 2026); claims a $1B company-led investment in missile-defense technology; **partnered with Apex (June 2026) for a 2027 on-orbit space-based-interceptor demonstration.** (V)
- Partner on SpaceX's classified NRO Starshield constellation (payload/manufacturing cooperation). (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- T3TRK award Dec 2025 ($764M, 18 satellites). (V)
- Apex SBI partnership announced 2026-06-01; on-orbit demo targeted 2027. (V)
- Ongoing T2TL-Alpha production against the same late-2026 launch window as York's 62. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why critical:** the purest apples-to-apples rival; SDA deliberately structured Alpha as a two-vendor race, so York's schedule, quality, and cost are benchmarked against Northrop every quarter. Its breadth (tracking + interceptors + payload partnerships) exceeds York's.
- **York counter-position:** York won the larger Alpha share (62 vs 38) at a lower per-satellite price, and delivered Tranche 1 to orbit first. The Apex partnership shows Northrop borrowing agility from a startup, validating York's model while also arming a rising competitor. (I)

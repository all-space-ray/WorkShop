# ThinKom Solutions

> **Threat rank: 2 of 10 (HIGH).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Hawthorne, CA. Established leader in mobility SATCOM antennas, best known for patented VICTS (Variable Inclination Continuous Transverse Stub) technology, a steerable mechanically-actuated phased array with an ultra-low profile that tracks satellites in any orbit. Deep aviation IFC install base, now pushing hard into contested-environment defense ground stations and digital arrays. (V)

## Technology and products

- **VICTS:** conformal, low-profile, mechanically-actuated phased array; precision steering and rapid beam agility; any orbital regime. Not a pure solid-state ESA (mechanical elements), which is its main architectural difference from All.Space/Kymeta. (V)
- **ThinAir Ka2517:** multi-orbit Ka terminal; operational on SES Open Orbits (MEO + GEO). (V)
- **Containerized Digital Array:** software-defined, multi-beam / multi-orbit / multi-band / multi-network across LEO/MEO/GEO/HEO; C, Ku, K, Ka (comm + mil), Q now; L/S, X, EO-Ka, V in development; low visual/heat/wind signature for a proliferated, survivable ground segment. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **$3.9M DoD portable ground stations** (follow-on, Feb 2026; up from an earlier $1.9M SBIR): airline-checkable phased-array gateways for dismounted forces, any orbit. (V)
- **$1.9M SpaceWERX D2P2 SBIR** (Jan 2026): containerized transportable phased-array ground entry point for the Department of the Air Force. (V)
- Ongoing SES multi-orbit service partnership via ThinAir Ka terminals. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why high:** proven and deployed at scale, genuinely multi-orbit, strong DoD + aviation credibility, moving into exactly the contested-environment ground/terminal niche All.Space targets. 
- **All.Space counter-position:** VICTS is mechanical-hybrid and oriented to single high-gain beams; All.Space's solid-state simultaneous dual-link full-duplex and lens anti-jam filtering are differentiators for the most contested tactical use. ThinKom's strength is mobility/aero and rapidly-deployable gateways. (I)

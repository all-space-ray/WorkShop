# Boeing / Millennium Space Systems

> **York threat rank: 6 of 10 (MEDIUM-HIGH).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

El Segundo smallsat prime (founded 2001, Boeing subsidiary since 2018, ~80% vertically integrated, LEO/MEO/GEO) with the strongest position in fire-control-grade missile tracking, the mission set Golden Dome funds most heavily. CEO Tony Gingiss (since Dec 2024) is scaling production from 1-2 satellites/month toward 6-12. (V)

## Technology and products

- Small satellite prime across orbits with active production lines and deep vertical integration. (V)
- **FOO Fighter constellation:** 8 satellites with fire-control-quality IR sensors (payloads from L3Harris) to track hypersonic and ballistic threats; includes ground system and mission ops. (V)
- ~$1B order for **12 MEO missile-tracking satellites** (US Space Force resilient MW/MT), plus classified programs. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **FOO Fighter ($414M SDA OTA, Apr 2024):** CDR completed in 10 months (Apr 2025); production underway; delivery window 2026 with launch targeted late 2027 per program updates; new 17,000 sqft dedicated facility. (V)
- Production capacity expansion across the El Segundo campus (2025-2026). (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium-high:** owns the fire-control tracking niche where Golden Dome money concentrates; Boeing capital; proven rapid design cycles (10-month CDR). Not a Transport Layer rival today.
- **York counter-position:** York's scale (126+ satellites) and cost position dwarf Millennium's boutique volumes; but if fire-control constellations become the growth engine, Millennium is positioned where York is not. Watch for Millennium bidding SDN/transport work or scaling beyond 8-12 satellite batches. (I)

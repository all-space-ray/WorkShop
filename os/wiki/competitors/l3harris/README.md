# L3Harris Technologies

> **All.Space threat rank: 9 of 10 (MEDIUM, frenemy).** **York threat rank: 5 of 10 (HIGH)**, see the [York section](#as-a-york-space-systems-competitor-high-rank-5-of-10) below. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Melbourne, FL. A defense prime that owns the modem layer of protected tactical SATCOM (Navy WAM; Air Force/Army A3M with the Protected Tactical Waveform) and builds terminals and multi-orbit systems (DEUCSI). It is both an All.Space integration partner (integrating modems such as WAM into the All.Space antenna, per prior program work) and a system-level competitor with the scale to integrate or backward-compete at the terminal level. (V/A)

## Technology and products

- **Modems:** WAM (Navy wideband anti-jam modem), A3M (Air Force/Army anti-jam modem, PTW). Owns critical protected-waveform modem IP the terminal layer depends on. (V)
- **Terminals/systems:** multi-orbit terminal development under AFRL DEUCSI; ground multiband terminals; early Amazon Leo partner. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium (frenemy):** as a prime that supplies the modem inside tactical terminals, L3Harris can shape terminal integration, bundle its own antenna/terminal solutions, and compete for the whole system while also being a partner and customer. The relationship is genuinely two-sided.
- **All.Space counter-position:** All.Space integrates L3Harris modems (WAM) and benefits from the partnership; the risk is L3Harris favoring an in-house or partner antenna. Manage as a key partner while monitoring for competitive terminal moves. (I)

## As a York Space Systems competitor (HIGH, rank 5 of 10)

L3Harris is also a satellite prime competing directly with York in the SDA marketplace, sourced in the [York research log](../../../raw/competitors/2026-07-10-york-competitive-landscape-research.md):

- **Tracking Layer incumbent across every tranche:** 4 Tranche 0 satellites on orbit; 14 T1TRK; 18 T2TRK (Jan 2024 cohort); **18 T3TRK missile warning/tracking satellites ($843M, Dec 2025)**; 34 satellites in development across T1/T2; expanded satellite integration and test facility in Palm Bay, Florida. Supplies the FOO Fighter IR payloads to Millennium Space. (V)
- **Why high for York:** L3Harris dominates the Tracking side of the PWSA, where York has no prime position, exactly where Golden Dome budgets concentrate; prime-scale resources; Aerojet Rocketdyne propulsion makes it a supplier to many satellite primes as well. (V/I)
- **York counter-position:** York leads on Transport heritage and cost/speed; L3Harris has never competed York head-to-head on a Transport award. The frenemy dynamic repeats at York level: L3Harris modems fly inside All.Space terminals even as L3Harris competes for the same architectures. (I)

# Lockheed Martin Space (incl. Terran Orbital)

> **York threat rank: 2 of 10 (CRITICAL).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

The largest defense prime in the SDA marketplace and York's most persistent head-to-head rival: Lockheed Martin has won satellites in every PWSA Transport and Tracking competition York has entered, states it is on contract for **124 SDA space vehicles**, and since late 2024 owns Terran Orbital, giving it captive high-rate smallsat bus production. (V)

## Technology and products

- **SDA satellite lines** built at its SmallSat Processing & Delivery Center in Colorado, using Terran Orbital buses (Ambassador and Renegade lines). (V)
- **Terran Orbital (wholly owned, late 2024):** Irvine, CA high-rate manufacturer; 350,000 sqft "Factory of the Future" moving to automated, 24/7 "automotive-style" production; delivered all 42 T1TL buses (Sep 2025); assembling 42 T2TL buses; selected Dec 2025 for 18 T3TRK buses. (V)
- Full prime-scale portfolio beyond smallsats: GPS, SBIRS/Next-Gen OPIR, classified programs, missile defense; one of 12 Golden Dome space-based-interceptor prototype awardees (April 2026). (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **SDA T3TRK (Dec 2025): $1.1B for 18 missile warning/tracking/defense satellites, the largest of the four T3 awards.** (V)
- SDA book across tranches: 42 T1TL; 36 T2TL-Beta ($816M, Aug 2023); Tranche 2 Tracking (18, Jan 2024 cohort); launched 21 T1TL satellites with 21 more in production. (V)
- Terran Orbital appointed a Senior Director of Manufacturing Operations (Mar 2026) to lead production scaling amid industry-wide SDA schedule scrutiny. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why critical:** matches York's Transport heritage, beats York in Tracking (where York has no prime position), owns its bus supply chain, and can absorb margin pressure indefinitely. In any Space Data Network on-ramp competition, Lockheed is the incumbent-scale bidder.
- **York counter-position:** York wins on speed and cost (Dragoon in 7 months; claimed half-cost position; first on orbit with Tranche 1) and on a leaner, purpose-built stack. Watch Terran Orbital's production ramp: if Lockheed matches York's cadence at prime scale, York's differentiation narrows to cost. (I)

# Apex Space

> **York threat rank: 8 of 10 (MEDIUM-HIGH, rising).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Los Angeles standardized-bus insurgent (founded 2022) explicitly aimed at proliferated constellations: **$720M+ raised (June 2026 round of $200M+ at a $2.3B valuation, the third ~$200M round in 14 months)**, Factory One production, and a Golden Dome strategy running through both self-funded demos and a Northrop Grumman partnership. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Bus lines:** Aries (launched SN1 March 2024; GEO variant), Nova, Comet: standardized platforms for 100-500 kg payloads across LEO/MEO/GEO/deep space. (V)
- **Insourcing:** Series D funded avionics, power, and propulsion internalization, including acquiring Phase Four's Hall-effect thruster technology. (V)
- **Project Shadow:** self-funded $15M space-based-interceptor demo: an "Orbital Magazine" with two scaled interceptor prototypes on a Nova bus; launch June 2026. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **$45.9M Space Systems Command contract (Feb 2025):** satellite deliveries through 2032 across multiple orbits, its largest government deal. (V)
- **Northrop Grumman SBI partnership (announced 2026-06-01):** Apex buses for Northrop's 2027 space-based-interceptor on-orbit demo. (V)
- Anduril partnership for AI-enabled space awareness/missile tracking. Not among the 12 April 2026 SBI prototype awardees (up to $3.2B pool); Project Shadow is its positioning bet for follow-on rounds. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium-high and rising:** purpose-built for constellation-scale production economics, heavily capitalized, and now attached to a prime's Golden Dome demo. It attacks York's core value proposition (standardized buses, speed, cost) from below.
- **York counter-position:** Apex has no program-of-record constellation deliveries; York has 126+ satellites of PWSA heritage, mission ops, and a full stack Apex lacks (ground, terminals, solar). The risk horizon is 2027+: if Project Shadow and the Northrop demo convert into production awards, Apex becomes a direct SDN-era rival. (I)

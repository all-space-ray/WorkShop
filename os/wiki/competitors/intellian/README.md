# Intellian Technologies

> **Threat rank: 7 of 10 (MEDIUM).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Seoul, South Korea. Global leader in satellite communication antennas and ground gateways with enormous manufacturing scale, historically dominant in maritime VSAT and now expanding aggressively into flat-panel ESA and government/defense. A new 20,575 sqm campus adds phased-array flat-panel, LEO user-terminal, and L-band production lines. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Flat Panel Series (AESA):** proprietary antenna element + custom vector-amplifier phased-array chipset for electronic beam control; includes a new agile Manpack solution; Ka-band multi-orbit flat panels previewed. (V)
- **ARC-M4 Block 1:** simultaneous X-band + Mil Ka + commercial Ka (WGS). **v130NX PM Dual-Ka (AN/USC-73):** electronically switches Mil/Comm Ka for maritime WGS resilience. (V)
- **ARC-M4-L24:** 2.4 m tactical WGS flyaway (COTP), claimed industry-first simultaneous X + Mil Ka flyaway. **OW11FA:** Ku LEO aero terminal with Panasonic Avionics (OneWeb, up to 195 Mbps). **Telesat Lightspeed** LEO terminal for fixed/enterprise/gov/land/maritime. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium:** vast manufacturing scale, deep maritime/WGS incumbency, aggressive flat-panel ESA expansion, and a "design reuse" strategy that lets it enter adjacent markets fast. 
- **All.Space counter-position:** Intellian's ESA multi-orbit Ka is still emerging and its heritage is mechanically-steered maritime; All.Space leads on simultaneous multi-orbit tactical resilience. Intellian's threat is breadth, scale, and price, especially in maritime/gov WGS. (I)

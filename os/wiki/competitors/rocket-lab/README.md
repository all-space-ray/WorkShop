# Rocket Lab (National Security / Space Systems)

> **York threat rank: 4 of 10 (HIGH).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

The closest "new prime" analog to York, with a launch segment York lacks. Rocket Lab (Nasdaq: RKLB) has amassed **$1.3B+ in SDA prime awards** in under two years and is vertically integrated from launch (Electron, HASTE, Neutron) through buses (Lightning, Flatellite), components, payloads (Geost IR sensors), and optical terminals (Mynaric). (V)

## Technology and products

- **Lightning bus:** selected for both SDA primes; high-volume production in Long Beach for 36+ SDA satellites. (V)
- **Flatellite (Feb 2025):** stackable, high-power constellation bus optimized for mass production and Neutron's fairing; aimed at proliferated national-security constellations. (V)
- **In-house supply chain:** reaction wheels, star trackers, separation systems, solar (SolAero heritage), radios; 2025 acquisitions of **Geost** (EO/IR payloads: Phoenix sensor) and **Mynaric** (laser optical comms terminals); StarLite protection sensors; InterMission ground software. (V)
- **Launch:** Electron (most-launched orbital small rocket); HASTE hypersonic testbed; **Neutron** medium-lift, first launch ~Q4 2026 (slipped after a January 2026 stage-test tank rupture). (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **SDA T3TRK (Dec 2025): ~$816M prime contract for 18 missile warning/tracking/defense satellites** (SDA lists $805M); its largest contract ever; passed System Requirements Review May 2026; company sees ~$1B total program capture including subsystem sales to other T3 primes. (V)
- SDA T2TL-Beta (Jan 2025): $515M for 18 satellites, launching no later than July 2027; in production. (V)
- $190M MACH-TB 2.0 hypersonic test contract (Mar 2026); Victus Haze responsive launch inside 17 hours (June 2026, $32M). (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why high:** SDA's newest prime is winning where York is absent (Tracking Layer), owns launch and optical terminals in-house, and its Flatellite/Neutron combination is engineered for exactly the proliferated-constellation economics the Space Data Network era rewards.
- **York counter-position:** York has deeper Transport heritage (126+ satellites vs 36), five years of on-orbit PWSA operations, and delivered Tranche 1 first. Rocket Lab's Neutron slip shows execution risk in its stack. Watch whether Rocket Lab bids Transport/SDN with Flatellite, which would put it squarely into York's lane. (I)

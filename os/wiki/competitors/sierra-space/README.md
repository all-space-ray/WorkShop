# Sierra Space

> **York threat rank: 7 of 10 (MEDIUM-HIGH).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Colorado neighbor converting commercial heritage into a defense-tech prime: **$1.5B in national security contracts since 2023 for 30 satellites**, a dedicated Sierra Space Defense unit (chartered June 2025), the Victory Works high-rate factory, and fresh capital (**$550M Series C at an $8B valuation, March 2026**). (V)

## Technology and products

- **Eclipse satellite bus line**, produced at Victory Works (Centennial, CO; 60,000 sqft dedicated defense facility within 1M+ sqft total infrastructure across seven states). (V)
- **Ghost:** precision re-entry/space delivery system. **Spectre:** rendezvous-proximity-operations satellite. Spacecraft subsystems business (20+ active contracts for solar arrays and components). Dream Chaser spaceplane (demo flight planned late 2026). (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **SDA Tranche 2 Tracking Layer: 18 missile warning/tracking satellites (max value $740M, Jan 2024).** Completed the first nine satellite structures three months ahead of schedule (Jan 2026). (V)
- $450M award for 4+ satellites for an unnamed National Security customer; Space Force Resilient GPS (R-GPS) "Quick Start" agreement (Sep 2024); CDRs completed for two major natsec programs of record (2025). (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium-high:** a tracking-layer prime with momentum, local talent competition (Colorado), and capital to keep bidding; R-GPS positions it in PNT, adjacent to York's assured-positioning thesis.
- **York counter-position:** Sierra's satellite volumes remain a fraction of York's, and its defense unit is young; York's operational track record (on-orbit deliveries, mission ops) is far deeper. Watch T2TRK integration/test execution and whether Sierra bids SDN or transport-class work with Eclipse. (I)

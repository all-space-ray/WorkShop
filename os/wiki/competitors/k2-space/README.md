# K2 Space

> **York threat rank: 9 of 10 (MEDIUM, rising).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Torrance, CA builder of large, high-power "Mega Class" satellites (founded 2022 by ex-SpaceX brothers Karan and Neel Kunjur). **$450-500M+ raised ($250M Series C at a $3B valuation, Dec 2025) and $500M-$1B+ in signed contracts**, including a Pentagon program of record (bus provider for Protected Tactical Satcom-Global) and 28 satellites for SES's future MEO network. Its bet: the heavy-lift era rewards bigger, higher-power satellites at disruptive cost, which collides directly with York's M-CLASS. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Mega Class:** ~2-ton satellite, 40 m solar wingspan, ~20 kW power (~10x class peers); multi-orbit hardened (LEO/MEO/GEO/cislunar); redundancy techniques borrowed from human-rated vehicles; flies on Falcon 9, Vulcan, Ariane 6. (V)
- **Dual-channel Hall thrusters** claimed as the most powerful currently flying; 85% of the vehicle built in-house; 180,000 sqft factory sized for ~100 satellites/year by 2030. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **Gravitas launched 2026-03-30:** first Mega Class production satellite; 100 days of operations, 20 kW arrays deployed, both Hall thrusters fired, 13 payloads (including DoD) exercised. First satellite, Heritage, flew Jan 2025. (V)
- **Protected Tactical Satcom-Global (PTS-G):** Pentagon program of record as bus provider. **SES:** 28 satellites for a future MEO network. 11 launches planned across 2026-2027; operational constellations from 2028. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium and rising:** different sweet spot (large/high-power vs York's proliferated smallsats) but converging on the same defense budgets: PTS-G, Golden Dome-era power-hungry payloads, and MEO constellations. M-CLASS (1,000+ kg, 8 kW) sits inside Mega Class's shadow on power.
- **York counter-position:** York has flown 126+ mission satellites and operates constellations; K2 has flown two experimental vehicles. York's common architecture spans three size classes with shared supply chain; K2 is single-platform. Watch PTS-G execution and whether K2's cost claims survive production. (I)

# CesiumAstro

> **Threat rank: 3 of 10 (HIGH).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Austin, TX. Developer of software-defined active phased arrays for space and defense (product lines include Skylark, Vireo, Nightingale). Very well capitalized (~$470M combined equity and debt raised) and scaling production. Historically strongest in satellite payloads and arrays; expanding into mobile-ready terminals, which is the vector that makes it an All.Space threat. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Scalable active phased array architecture:** true solid-state AESA, software-defined, modular from small to large aperture; mobile-ready terminals adaptable from ground vehicles to airborne assets. (V)
- **Skylark** product line expanded 2026 with a large-aperture array variant. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **MDA GMD Weapon System (GWS) IFICS** (April 21, 2026): awarded to deliver a new large-aperture active phased array (Skylark expansion) supporting Ground-based Midcourse Defense modernization. Signals defense-grade credibility and large-aperture capability. (V)
- Founder/CEO Shey Sabripour. Standardized platforms across satellites, payloads, terminals, and computing for scalable production. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why high:** genuine active phased array technology, exceptional funding, defense momentum, and a stated push into mobile terminals. If it turns array leadership into multi-orbit tactical terminals, it competes squarely with Hydra and Kymeta.
- **All.Space counter-position:** CesiumAstro's heritage is payloads/arrays rather than fielded multi-orbit mobility terminals; All.Space has a shipping tactical terminal and multi-orbit certifications today. Watch for a CesiumAstro multi-orbit ground terminal announcement. (I)

# Hanwha Phasor

> **Threat rank: 4 of 10 (MEDIUM-HIGH).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

London, UK; a division of Korean aerospace/defense group Hanwha. Builds the A7700 active electronically steered antenna (AESA), a modular multi-beam, constellation-agnostic flat-panel terminal. Ku-band first (market entry ~2025), with Ka planned via technology reuse. Aero-first but scalable to land and naval. Hanwha's defense backing and capital make it a durable competitor. (V)

## Technology and products

- **A7700 AESA:** modular square-tile phased array, scalable to platform size (howitzer to ship to airliner); two receive beams (company calls this "fairly unique"); constellation-agnostic (GEO + LEO); resilient to jamming/interference with channel/satellite switching. Earlier cryogenic-cooling requirement was redesigned out; ~120 mm height. (V)
- **Ka-band:** planned after Ku, reusing Ku development, targeted to arrive alongside Ka LEO networks (Rivada, Telesat). (V)

## Recent activity and partnerships

- **Eutelsat OneWeb:** terminal qualification for the LEO constellation. (V)
- **Lufthansa Technik:** radomes + EASA/FAA supplemental type certification (EASA targeted Q3 2025, FAA Q4 2025). (V)
- **Kontron:** power-component partnership (multi-year, tens of millions GBP). **Plexus:** contract manufacturing (UK) for scale/de-risking. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium-high:** Hanwha capital + defense pedigree, modular scalability across domains, dual-Rx-beam differentiation, and aero certification progress. 
- **All.Space counter-position:** Hanwha is Ku-first and aero-led; All.Space leads on Mil-Ka and simultaneous multi-orbit tactical today. The threat rises when Hanwha's Ka product and land/naval variants mature. (I)

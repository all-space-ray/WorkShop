# Muon Space

> **York threat rank: 10 of 10 (MEDIUM, rising).** Competes with: York Space Systems. As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Mountain View end-to-end space systems company (founded 2021) built around the **Halo** stack: spacecraft platforms, payload integration, and a software-defined orchestration layer, the same "full mission lifecycle" pitch York makes, at smaller scale but compounding fast (>100% YoY growth two years running, headcount doubled, $146M Series B). (V)

## Technology and products

- **Halo:** integrated spacecraft + payload management + software-defined orchestration for real-time on-orbit compute and decision-making; Silicon Valley production facilities. (V)
- Vertical integration push: acquired propulsion startup **Starlight Engines** (zinc-based thrusters). (V)
- Mission lines: FireSat (wildfire IR constellation with Earth Fire Alliance), Quickbeam multispectral IR payloads, RF collection buses for SNC's Vindler 2.0, Hydrosat thermal missions. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **MDA SHIELD IDIQ (awarded Dec 2025/announced Jan 2026): on the $151B-ceiling multi-award vehicle for next-generation missile defense**, giving it a contracting path into Golden Dome-era work. (V)
- **$44.6M Space Force SBIR Phase III OTA (Oct 2025):** three-satellite dual-use environmental monitoring demo (cloud characterization, theater weather) with enhanced Quickbeam payloads. (V)
- NRO Stage II commercial electro-optical contract (2025); $1.9M SpaceWERX D2P2 supporting SDA's MW/MT mission; three operational FireSats launching mid-2026; SNC Vindler 2.0 first three satellites Q1 2026. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium and rising:** software-led constellation prime with a live path into missile-defense sensing (SHIELD) and weather/EO niches SDA-adjacent budgets fund; its Halo pitch mirrors York's integrated-stack story for a new generation of programs.
- **York counter-position:** Muon's satellites are small-batch scientific/sensing missions; York operates defense constellations at two orders of magnitude more delivered mass. The risk is niche capture: SHIELD task orders and SBEM programs could grow Muon into the "commercial sensing layer" of architectures York wants to prime. (I)

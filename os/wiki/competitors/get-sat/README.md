# Get SAT

> **Threat rank: 6 of 10 (MEDIUM).** As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Israel (with US operations). Specialist in compact, all-in-one military Ka-band electronically steered terminals for airborne ISR, tactical airlift, and command-and-control. Its Aero Blade M line is notably smaller and lighter than All.Space's Hydra, making it a strong competitor where low SWaP is the deciding factor. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Aero Blade M SOLO Ka:** next-gen ESA, no moving parts, integrated modem/ACU/frequency-converter/tracking/RF; GEO + MEO + LEO with switching; Tx 27.6-31 GHz, Rx 17.7-21.2 GHz; max EIRP 53 dBW; G/T 12.5 dB/K; 360-deg azimuth, 15-90 el; ~23 kg; DO-160 airborne qualification. (V)
- **Aero Blade M PICO Ka:** ultra-compact (1.6 kg) dual-beam Rx ESA; modem-agnostic; GEO/MEO/LEO. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why medium:** leading compact/low-SWaP mil-Ka ESA for air and tactical edge; integrated single-box design; established defense customers.
- **All.Space counter-position:** Get SAT is mostly single-link airborne/tactical, not simultaneous multi-orbit dual-full-duplex; All.Space wins where multi-link resilience and higher aggregate throughput matter, Get SAT wins on size/weight for smaller airborne platforms. Different sweet spots that overlap in airborne tactical. (I)

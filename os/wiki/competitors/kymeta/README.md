# Kymeta Corporation

> **Threat rank: 1 of 10 (CRITICAL).** Competes with: All.Space (and York). As of 2026-07-10.

## Snapshot

Redmond, WA; founded 2012. The world's leading flat-panel satellite terminal manufacturer, built on metamaterial-based electronically steered antennas ("Intelligent Communications Platforms"), 150+ patents. The single most direct threat to All.Space: overlapping multi-band/multi-orbit ESA product, deeper current DoD program momentum, and a lower-SWaP flat-panel form factor. (V)

## Technology and products

- **Metamaterial ESA:** software-defined flat-panel arrays; no moving parts; hybrid satellite+cellular; edge processing. (V)
- **KuKa 8 Series:** first multi-band (Ku 10.7-14.5 GHz + Ka 17.7-31.0 GHz), multi-orbit (GEO/MEO/LEO/HEO), single-aperture flat-panel ESA; four concurrent beams (2 Tx + 2 Rx) from one metamaterial surface (demonstrated June 2025, TRL6); 35.4 x 35.4 x 3.9 in; dual-modem. Prototypes to key customers mid-2026; commercial availability 2027. Positioned as the smallest/lightest simultaneous dual-LEO multi-orbit multi-band flat panel on the market. (V)

## Recent activity and contracts

- **US Army NGC2 (Next Generation Command and Control) pilot:** selected as multi-orbit SATCOM provider, Q3 2025. (V)
- **ONR contract via Bascom Hunter (2026-04-14):** deliver the first single-antenna multi-band multi-orbit SATCOM terminal for US Navy testing from Q2 2026. **This is the same prime (Bascom Hunter) and Navy relationship as All.Space's STtNG program** - direct account collision. (V)
- **Leadership:** Manny Mora appointed President & CEO Nov 2025 (40-year General Dynamics Mission Systems veteran) specifically to align to DoD/JADC2. (V)

## Threat assessment

- **Why critical:** direct product overlap on multi-band/multi-orbit ESA; actively winning the exact US Navy/Army accounts All.Space pursues; more DoD traction now; flat-panel form factor undercuts Hydra on SWaP; new mil-focused CEO.
- **All.Space counter-position:** Hydra offers true simultaneous full-duplex dual-link today and stronger anti-jam lens filtering; Kymeta's KuKa is still prototype (mid-2026) to commercial (2027). Watch whether Kymeta ships on schedule and whether its four-beam claim matches Hydra's full-duplex-both-beams capability in the field. (I)
